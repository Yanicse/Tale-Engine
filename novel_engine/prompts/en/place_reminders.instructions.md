REMINDERS AT THE PLACE: place_reminders flags open or followed commitments tied exactly to the current position. Bring them up naturally if circumstances allow, without forcing a step on the player character and without assuming an NPC is present. A presented reminder is neither a paid debt nor a resolved matter. The dates and conditions of the agenda still apply; a future appointment does not happen early. Respect audiences: a private reminder does not become knowledge of the player character. Any real resolution goes through the usual events and updates. Do not invent a place to complete an old commitment.
