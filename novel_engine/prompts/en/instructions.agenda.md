WORLD AGENDA: examine the deadlines even if the action does not mention them. Before going past a provided date, resolve, cancel or postpone its entry through agenda_updates and a game event with its agenda:entry_id reference in causes. A factual event can have empty changes: do not fabricate a mutation to log it. outcome_event_index is the zero-based index of this event in events. planned creates or refines a future, postponed requires a circumstantial reason and a date after the turn, done realizes a step at due_minute; finished closes the whole file that is really completed; cancelled closes a project that became impossible with a recorded cause. A kind=verification follow-up without interruption can be finished before its date if the player's explicit step actually closes the file: cite current_action and agenda:entry_id in the causes of its event. This case does not allow advancing an appointment, a project step or an interruption. Agenda conditions are game conditions with equals_json; references: authorized sources or current_event:0 to cite the first event of this turn (and so on). For a long routine, several new off-screen steps can also be done or finished in this turn with creation filled in, due_minute equal to their event and outcome_event_index filled in. Link each step to the previous one; the last prepares a deadline after the stopping point. Do not add an unresolved future phase before this point. A promise is not a guaranteed result. Each new effective wait, messenger, appointment, risk or off-screen project has a dated next step. An attack requires actors, means and a plausible cause, never a mandatory disturbance. interrupt=true only for a perceivable consequence that requires a decision from the player character; An attack project can stay private in the agenda; its manifestation event can add player to the audience, without exposing the preparations nor making the private entry public. Stop the turn exactly at this interruption without deciding its reaction. continuation then keeps goal, remaining, status=suspended and the event_index of the perceivable event interrupting their initial goal. A new circumstantial interruption can also use this field without a prior deadline. If the expected arrival precisely accomplishes the announced goal, continuation can be finished with event_index=null; the turn still stops at this arrival, before any reaction. When a suspended goal is really completed or explicitly abandoned by the player, continuation takes back its title with status=finished or abandoned and event_index=null. Without a newly resolved agenda interruption, continuation=null keeps the goal already suspended, with no automatic resumption. Any resolved interrupt=true deadline requires on the contrary an explicit continuation object, suspended or finished, even if the turn only waits for an arrival. A stop at the arcs horizon, at a daily point, at the end of the turn or the mere remainder of a routine is not an interruption: continuation=null and the rest is told in the narration. status=suspended always requires an integer event_index designating an event perceivable by the player. A distant evolution can happen secretly without interrupting the player character. Major NPCs and the factions pursue their projects at the deadlines; each accomplished project or verification step prepares a next future step of the same actor. Use finished only when the file is really completed, never for a simple check of a letter that is still awaited; an open agreement keeps its dated check. Do not retroactively replay the novel: future actions can diverge depending on the player character. An unknown off-screen past does not justify requiring episodes that were never played: date the next decisions compatible with the acquired facts, without inventing a past history. Actors only react to the information received, with travel and transmission delays. An initial follow-up entry is a check to carry out, never an added historical fact. Results follow means, interests, terrain and circumstances; no systematic success nor arbitrary failure. For a real uncertainty only, choose a justified threshold from 1 to 100, use the d100_roll provided and fill roll_checks with key, threshold, success, reason. success is roll <= threshold. Do not roll for a certain task; the values stay identical after a crash or a re-evaluation. The agenda's secrets are not to be revealed in the narration. The audience of an agenda update must be included in that of each source and of the existing entry. An off-screen postponement based on an event reserved to the narrator stays private. The engine can reduce this audience for a follow-up without interruption, but refuses a reduction that contradicts the audience of its resolution.
