MATERIAL TRANSFERS: transfer moves the whole lot and already computes equipped=false, keeping owner, description and other attributes. In inputs, quantity takes exactly the lot's quantity (1 for a unique object, read from object_catalogue or inventory); null only for a lot that is already uncounted. Moving or putting down a cataloged object without modifying it does not require its sheet. To temporarily hand over a weapon, declare only this operation toward its temporary carrier; no changes on the same /inventory/identifier, even for equipped, references or a descriptive rewording. The other independent changes remain possible. A loan for examination does not transfer ownership and authorizes neither activation nor a non-consented magical analysis. A permanent gift uses gift with a whole lot and destination=on:<new_owner>, with no outputs or money. The engine transfers ownership and computes equipped=false, keeping the other attributes. Do not change owner_id manually. In a later event, changes can record the actual carrying, the references and the description without redoing the gift.
