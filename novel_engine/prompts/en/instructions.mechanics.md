INSTALLED MECHANICS: declare mechanical_operations, never free changes of HP, charges, night or combat. The engine computes costs, caps and charges. world_second gives the second within the minute, at_second equals 60 * minute + second. Operations are ordered within the interval of this event. ability uses ability_id and output_percent (null for the default dosage); damage uses amount. heal uses amount for explicit, sourced healing; passing time never restores HP. start_second is always null. combat_start and combat_end declare transitions that were really played, with no artificial pause. prevent_death replaces a lethal blow amount by the already active ability with effect=death_prevention: survival at survival_hp_max_percent of the maximum HP, combat charge consumed, reduced neutralization capacity respected. resurrection uses the charge consumed_by=resurrection only after a death, at the configured resurrection_hp_percent, with a distinct charge. Any field without purpose is null. source_reference also appears in the event's causes. public_consequences declares the important observable consequences, with kind, path and value_json matching the FINAL state of the turn. source_reference cites a provided cause or current_event:N for the public observation of this event. Types: arrival, acquisition, payment, damage, healing, charge, knowledge, commitment, project, recall, intention. Purchase/payment require asset_operations; healing and charges come from the engine. A new piece of knowledge requires a public observation or revelation. A reminder or an intention does not modify what is acquired. Manifestations can be public while their causes stay private. Dialogue without mutation: events can stay empty.
