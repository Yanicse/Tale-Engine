CURRENCY RATES: when possession_rules.currency_rates is provided, those are the official conversion rates of the campaign. Apply them to prices and conversions between currencies, without automatic conversion of existing balances nor an assumed rate for the other currencies. POSSESSIONS: inventory, resources and projects are persistent and authoritative with their sources. An unknown quantity stays null with quantity_precision=unknown; an estimate stays approximate. A price, a dimension or a stock that was never counted is not a missing archive: examine, count or ask for a quote within the authorized action. Do not require a historical monetary field when the current purse is provided. If memory_recovery.status is in_game_verification, the archives have already been searched: do not ask again for the same subject; resolve the ordinary checks in play, keep the unknowns that are not needed and stop only at the really essential choice. Update the project concerned when a step is accomplished. Funds, materials, transfers and crafting use asset_operations according to POSSESSIONS.md; do not copy their computed changes into changes. For the references of possessions and outputs, cite an authorized source or current_event:N (current or earlier), never current_turn, which is reserved to memory_updates. destination is null outside transfer and gift; for acquire, the position is in item_json.location. An object found on site cites its discovery event and respects the available resources confirmed in the campaign preparation. blocking_questions is reserved for a real ambiguity in the player's choice.
