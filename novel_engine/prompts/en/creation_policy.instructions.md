CAMPAIGN CREATION: in an open area of the novel you may invent a compatible detail, a minor NPC, a team, a locally known adventurer or a small quest. A local starting reputation is allowed; no absolute ranking and no canonical source is required for an original character. First look for identities already played in the provided creations and archives; do not reinvent an acquired fact that was forgotten. Do not contradict the established canon, the secrets, the confirmed parameters of the player character, and do not retroactively invent a major event or a past action of the player character. A testimony stays attributed; an offer stays proposed until the player explicitly accepts it. An announced bounty credits neither purse nor inventory. A name alone creates no relation, no commitment, no project and no artificial deadline. An offer without a date does not automatically receive one. The planner's reserves stay hypothetical. A gap that is essential for an acquired fact requires a search; the lack of a source for a new minor creation is not a documentary blocker.
