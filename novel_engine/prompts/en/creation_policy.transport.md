CREATIONS FORMAT 1: creation_updates declares each durable element with a stable entity_id (reuse the one provided), name, aliases, kind, origin=campaign, status, traits, unknowns, links, audience and exact references. Traits stay within the declared sources. Use current_turn for public dialogue, current_event:N for an event, otherwise an archive from memory_references. Each view has its own identity: a public revelation of a private file creates a linked public view without copying its secret aliases. All traits of a sheet share its status; split a feat that is only told into a reputation sheet with status testimony linked to the established NPC. A correction adds a sourced evolution without erasing the history. Quest states are proposed, accepted, resolved, refused or abandoned; accepted requires an explicit decision in the current action. A question or memory_request must have an empty creation_updates. No implicit payment or agenda change.
