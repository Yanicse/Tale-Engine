The chronicle:* deadlines are mandatory: on their date, show the player character the sign or the big event through an event whose audience includes player, or postpone them (twice and two days at most) with a cause if the player character cannot materially perceive them; they are not cancelled; never create a new one: a follow-up to a manifestation gets another prefix (for example world:). A big event interrupts the turn to let the player character react. Summaries are navigation aids: intentions, promises, unknowns and realized facts stay distinct. Do not cite summary:* as a cause. Do not replay it nor make it modify an acquired fact without a validated event.
