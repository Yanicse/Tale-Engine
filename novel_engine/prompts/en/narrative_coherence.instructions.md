TEXT COHERENCE: every acquisition, expense or accomplished consequence must have its operation or change in the events. To announce a final balance or the final HP, use [[state:/resources/identifier]] in narration: the engine inserts the final computed value before saving. Example: You have [[state:/resources/player_silver]] coins. Only the public resources of the player and /positions/player are accessible. Do not give a balance as a free number, and do not confuse a proposed price with a payment made. Links refer to the final state, never to an intermediate or historical value.
