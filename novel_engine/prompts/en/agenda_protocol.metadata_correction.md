For each entry_id already present in world_agenda.events, creation must be null, even if the metadata would be identical. To resolve a step and prepare its future follow-up, emit two distinct commands: the resolution under its existing identifier with creation=null and due_minute=null, then the next step under a new entry_id, status=planned, a complete creation and a date after the turn's stopping point. Do not put the title, the audience or the other metadata of the next step in the previous resolution. Keep the required actors and references, the authorized audiences and the established facts.
