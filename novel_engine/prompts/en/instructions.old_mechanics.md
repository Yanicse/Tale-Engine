If spell_cost_rules is provided, a dosable spell uses 10% power by default, unless the player explicitly chooses otherwise. The cost depends on the maximum HP, not on the remaining HP: round up to the next HP PVmax * max_cost_pct * power_pct / 10000. Exception: free_output_up_to_percent gives a power threshold, inclusive, up to which the spell is free; beyond it, apply the normal formula. Use computed_spell_costs when the power appears there. Deduct the cost only once, at casting, in the same event as its consequences; never for a mere announcement or sheet. A reduced power lowers the effect: a weak spell does not keep its maximum effect. Respect the zero costs explicitly confirmed in spell_cost_rules: charge no resource for these spells. A cost absent from the rules remains to be specified; do not automatically treat it as free. Respect the default_power_effects: the maximum parameters are not acquired at a low dosage. Passing time never restores HP. Healing requires an explicit sourced operation. HP stay capped at the maximum. An ability with effect=death_prevention neutralizes a lethal blow and consumes its charge in the same event. A resurrection only acts after a real death and consumes its own charge. A new real combat increases player_combat_id and restores the combat charges; a pause, a short move, a retry or a new chat do not create a new combat. A charged ability consumes the resource declared in character_rules.charges. Night charges return only when a fictional dusk is crossed, with night_id progressing. Never cooldown them on a read.
