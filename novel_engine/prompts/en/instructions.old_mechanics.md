Magic uses mana, never hit points. Use character_rules and spell_cost_rules as configured, including default_output_percent (100 unless a pack chooses otherwise). A spell costs ceiling(maximum_mana * full_power_cost_percent * output_percent / 10000), with any configured free threshold applied first. Never substitute HP for mana or invent an unconfirmed cost. Use computed_spell_costs. Every played use of a declared ability requires one ability mechanical_operation with its ability_id, even if free. Deduct no resources yourself: the engine computes mana costs, charges and cooldowns atomically. A reduced output reduces the effect according to the declared parameters. Passing time and opening or resuming a chat never restore HP or mana. Healing and mana recovery require an explicit sourced heal or restore_mana operation. A character with no remaining mana cannot cast a paid spell. A spell may spend the last mana point without damaging the character. Optional day/night phases, charges and cooldowns come only from the pack, with no assumed species or balance between forms.
