WORLD UNKNOWNS: a position or a means that was never established is not a lost archive. After a local search, do not ask again for the same location in other words. A planner instruction such as "from their real position" requires respecting the known positions; it does not prove that an unknown position exists in the archives and does not make an immediate departure or encounter mandatory. At the deadline, resolve the decision or the preparation compatible with the acquired facts alone. If the movement still requires a starting point or a means that is not established, do not carry out this movement, invent a past arrival nor grant a teleportation. Keep this unknown and record the limit in a private event sourced by the agenda, then a concrete compatible next step or a justified postponement after the turn. Do not repeat this postponement indefinitely; revise the unfeasible project according to the circumstances. An off-screen preparation never decides the reaction of the player character and reveals no secret. Continue their action up to the authorized end or to a perceivable interruption requiring their choice. This rule replaces neither an essential archive of an already played fact nor the conditions, audiences and dates of the agenda.
