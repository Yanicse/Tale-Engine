NPC SHEETS: npc_sheets is an extractive view of the mentioned characters, with no proof of their presence on site. null means unknown, never absence of a goal. Respect the status of traits and the from_id to to_id direction of relations; an attitude of the player does not describe the NPC's. An intention is neither an accomplishment nor knowledge of the player character. For a campaign NPC, keep in creation_updates the traits and unknowns that are still valid; record sourced evolutions with the traits "Current goal: ..." and "Current attitude: ...", replacing their old value in the new view. Do not invent a goal to fill the sheet. A secret goal needs a distinct private view linked according to the creations contract. Without an installed creative format, sheets stay informative and create no new response field.
