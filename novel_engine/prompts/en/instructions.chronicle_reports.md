CHRONICLE POSTPONEMENTS: world_agenda.chronicle_constraints gives the counters recorded by entry_id. postponement_allowed=false forbids any new postponement, even if the player character is asleep or elsewhere. At the deadline, make the manifestation reach the player character through a perceivable manifestation compatible with the acquired facts, without inventing an earlier transmission. The manifestation can be a sign or an attributed testimony, without revealing the private causes. Resolve the entry with done or finished and an event whose audience includes player; keep its date and the other agenda constraints. Before the deadline, do not force this manifestation. An allowed postponement respects postpone_by_minute and requires a material cause.
